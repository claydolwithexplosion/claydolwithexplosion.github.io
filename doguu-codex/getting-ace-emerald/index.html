Getting ACE in Pokemon Emerald

To get started with ACE, there is only really one glitch: Glitzer Popping/Pomeg Corruption. This glitch can be done without beating the game, but it's HIGHLY recommended to do it post-game, because it makes the chance of success after each try a multitude higher.

Getting the trades

To get started, you need to get some specific in-game trade Pokemon. The Pokemon we want to get are specific, you can either get

  • the "DOTS" (Seedot) from Rustboro City for a Ralts
  • the "SEASOR" (Horsea) from Pacifidlog Town for a Bagon

If you've already gotten one of them and they have gained any experience already, you most definitely want to get the other one to be safe.

Once you've gotten one of the working species, DON'T enter any battle, just go to the PC and deposit them there to be safe.

You'll also want to get the in-game trade "PLUSES", which can be gotten at Fortree City for a Volbeat. Volbeat is a very rare encounter west of Mauville (1%!), but you can also get an Illumise and breed it with a compatible Pokemon (Ditto for example), and the eggs will have a 50% chance of being a Volbeat.

PLUSES isn't strictly needed, but it improves the odds quite a bit.

If the PLUSES already gained experience and/or EVs, that shouldn't matter, but you'll have to remove all moves except for Growl (relearn it in Fallarbor Town at the move relearner if needed), so that Growl is its only move. Also just store it safely in the PC for now.

Making a clone

Now, to be safe and learn about the cloning glitch, you should now make a clone of the fresh DOTS/SEASOR. To do the cloning glitch, you need access to the Battle Tower, that's why this method is post-game only.

The cloning glitch is actually really simple, though you have to follow the right order, otherwise you might accidentally delete (un-clone) the Pokemon you are trying to clone. So first, go to the Battle Tower (at the north-east of the Battle Frontier) and go to the PC in the top right. Then:

  1. Make sure, that you have at least one free slot in your party. If you want to clone more than one Pokemon at once, you should have the same amount of free slots as you want to clone.
  2. Save in front of the PC.
  3. Open the PC and withdraw the Pokemon you want to clone.
  4. Go to the lady that's the furthest to the right and talk to her as if you want to enter a battle, so choose some Pokemon to fight (it doesn't matter, which you choose).
  5. She'll ask you, whether you want to save the game once, but there shouldn't be the normal save window open (with the playtime and all). Say yes here.
  6. Now, the regular save prompt should appear, asking you to save again. Just say no here.

Aaaand you're basically done. Now you just need to reset the game, either with a hardreset (with a button on the console), or a soft reset (by pressing A+B+Start+Select at the same time). When you load back into the game, you'll stand in front of the lady, and the cloned Pokemon will be both in your party and the PC.

If you're worried, because this uses saving and quitting, don't be, this has no chance to corrupt your save file if you do it as described (especially with a soft reset, you can't even reset during a save). This exploit doesn't make use of the saving mechanism itself, but just uses the stupidity of the programmers, because they implemented a partial save for the battle tower (aka, it explicitly saves ONLY your party and location, but not the PC storage).

Cloning items

Cloning items follows a similar principle and is relatively easy. In principle you could clone items by just cloning Pokemon which are holding an item, however that way you can only clone 5 or 6 items at a time.

This method can clone as many items as you have Pokemon in your PC, so if you have a full box you can clone an item 30 times relatively quickly.

  1. To get started, put at least as many Pokemon as you want clones of the item into one box (make sure they aren't holding items themselves).
  2. Either from one of the Pokemon moving modes, or with the Item moving mode in the PC, give the item(s) to as many Pokemon as you can.
  3. Exit the PC, and save.
  4. Go back into the PC, and with your preferred mode, take the item(s) you want to clone from the Pokemon you gave the item(s) to.
  5. Talk to the lady on the right, and proceed as before when cloning Pokemon.

Once you reload the save, you'll have the item(s) both in the bag, and the Pokemon in the PC will still be holding the items.

To prepare the rest of the setup, it's recommended to clone some HP Ups (5 or so), especially if you dont's have the money to buy enough.

Doing the EV training

What are EVs?

EVs, short for Effort Values, are values that each Pokemon has, starting in Generation 3. Each Pokemon has one Effort Value for each of HP, ATK, DEF, SP.ATK, SP.DEF and SPEED. Each value goes from 0 to 255, and for every 4 EVs that a Pokemon has in one stat, it gets one stat point more at level 100, with lower levels having the scaled value.

You can increase the EVs of a Pokemon either by defeating other Pokemon (every time a Pokemon gets experience, it also gets EVs), and with vitamins (HP Up, Proteine etc.), which each increase the respective EV by 10. If a Pokemon has Pokerus, or had Pokerus and was cured, it gets double EVs from battles. If a Pokemon is holding the Macho Brace while getting the EVs, the EVs from battle also get doubled. What kind of EV you get from defeating a Pokemon depends on the Pokemon you defeat, so defeating a Whismur will always give a base EV of 1HP EV. What Pokemon gives what EVs is available in most Pokemon databases.

Now that you have a clone of the untouched DOTS/SEASOR, you need to do the EV training, to get an egg, that will execute ACE upon hatching (called the "unstable" ACE species).

Be VERY CAREFUL here, as the EVs are a hidden value, there is no way to read them without ACE, and you won't know until you hatch the egg, whether you did the EV training right (so until the very end).

There are a few unstable ACE species that exist in every language (except Japanese), I'll be covering 0x0611 (the traditional one). To get this 0x0611, you need to get the DOTS/SEASOR to have exactly:

  • 6 Attack EVs
  • 17 HP EVs

The easiest way to give the DOTS/SEASOR the needed EVs is by training with the Exp. Share or by giving it vitamins. So, to get the Attack EVs, you can:

  1. Go to Mt. Pyre (the big mountain southwest of Lilicove)
  2. Give the Exp. Share to the DOTS/SEASOR inside there, and defeat exactly 6 Shuppets (you can count PP)
  3. Take off the Exp. Share and leave

To defeat exactly 6 Shuppets and be sure, it is recommended to defeat them with a strong Pokemon with a powerful 100% accuracy move that can one-hit-KO all the Shuppets, because you can just count the PP. For example, if you have a Swampert with Surf, and you have 15 PP before, you can just defeat Shuppets until you only have 9 PP left. Since inside Mt. Pyre there are only Shuppets, you don't need to worry about other encounters either.

The HP EVs are a slightly different story:

  1. Fly to Slateport City, go to the market on the left (or go to the Lilicove Department store on the right floor)
  2. Buy one HP Up (ideally a few more for the upcoming glitch)
  3. Give that one HP Up to the DOTS/SEASOR
  4. Fly to Verdanturf, enter the Rusturf tunnel
  5. Put on the Exp. Share on the DOTS/SEASOR, and defeat exactly 7 Whismurs (again, count PP to be safe)
  6. Take off the Exp. Share and leave

(Instead of buying and giving the HP Up, you can also defeat 17 Whismurs instead of 7 Whismurs, but the HP Up saves some time.)

Now the EV training part is done, so put the EV-trained DOTS/SEASOR into the PC. To not mix it up with the original one, you can always look at its experience and/or level, the clean DOTS/SEASOR should be lower level and/or have less experience.

Preparing the corruption

Now that you have the EV trained DOTS/SEASOR and the PLUSES with only Growl prepared, it's time to prepare the rest of the setup, by making 5 clones of the EV trained DOTS/SEASOR, and 4 clones of the PLUSES. Use the cloning exploit, as mentioned above.

Then, the last step is to clear Boxes 1-2 (move the Pokemon in there to the later boxes), and arrange the clones of the EV trained DOTS/SEASOR and PLUSES like this:

- - - - - -
P D P D P D
P D P D - D
- - - - - -
Where "P" stands for the PLUSES with only Growl, and "D" stands for the EV trained DOTS/SEASOR. This is the optimal setup with the PLUSES, which has a probability of 7/32. If you didn't get the PLUSES, this is the best setup I found: 
- - - - - -
- - - - - -
- - - - - -
- - - D D D
- - - - - -
which has a probability of 2/32, so quite a bit lower, but still double that of not using clones.

Doing the Pomeg Corruption

This is the part, where you do the actual game-breaking stuff, so a save before this point is good to be safe. The glitch (the pomeg corruption, glitzer popping, or whatever you want to call it) is very useful, but can also mess with your save file if you don't follow the instructions. If you follow all instructions, there is not anything to worry about though.

Now, as the name suggests, you need at least one, ideally two or more Pomeg berries. You can find them in a few places, like west of Fortree City, or right outside the Berry Master's house, or you can even get it from the daily berry master event (with some luck or saving and resetting). For convenience, a few HP Ups (two or three) are also needed.

In its core, the glitch relies on an oversight regarding the Pomeg berry. The Pomeg berry lowers HP EVs, but gives a Pokemon more friendship. However, if you have a Pokemon with 1 HP left, and the lowering of the HP EVs causes the HP stat to decrease, you can faint the Pokemon that way outside of battle. That way, the game doesn't recognize that you actually fainted the Pokemon, so you can still walk around with the whole party fainted.
