# TorchicOS
>So you've made it. You have a stable way to trigger ACE, you have a base64 writer to generate whatever Pokemon you want, you have a plethora of codes right at your fingertips.
>This guide is for the people who want even more, who want to take ACE in Generation 3 to its absolute limits…

Requirements:
- A stable ACE species, which means
  - in Ruby/Sapphire: 0x44fc Celebi ACE
  - in FR/LG: any grab ACE species really (0x351 recommended)
  - in Emerald: any stable or metastable ACE species
- A modern base64 writer, so either WELDR, or at least the bad egg base64 writer 3.0

TorchicOS is a sophisticated setup for improving the possibilities with ACE in these games, but its main idea is being *modular*. The basic setup consists only of ~3-4 Pokemon, but you can extend this to your needs, and "insert" modules as you go.

The basic way of interacting with TorchicOS is through the "terminal", which is a payload that hooks into the in-game naming screen. The terminal accepts commands in a specific format:
```
some data to be used . control letters
```

There are different naming screens in the game, one for entering your trainer name, one for naming boxes and one for nicknaming Pokemon. Additionally, exclusively in Pokemon Emerald, there is the naming screen for the Walda phrase, in Rustboro City. Thus, TorchicOS has two different terminals:
- TRQIC, which uses the Pokemon nicknaming screen (10 characters per screen)
- T, which uses the Walda phrase screen (15 characters per screen)

However, both can be used in almost the same way, just the input will be split differently, 30 input characters would be split into 3 nicknames with TRQIC and 2 walda phrases with T.

You can enter as many characters as you need into the terminal, by just filling the naming screen and confirming the message, you'll get greeted with another empty naming screen where you can continue the command. Also, the terminal has a checksum mechanism, which means that after each naming screen (so every 10 or 15 letters), you'll get a "checksum" in the form of a Pokemon icon (TRQIC) or a specific letter (T).

Generally, all characters can be used for commands, but there are two special characters; the dot (`.`) and the minus (`-`).
- The dot should be entered at the end of the data section, after it comes the control letters of the command, which specify which modules should be used for this command. That way, you can actually combine modules in different ways.
- The minus should be entered only if you see that your checksum of your previous naming screen was wrong. Once you type a minus and confirm the naming screen, you'll be sent back to the previous naming screen, where you can redo that part.

The terminal is one of 3 Pokemon which you'll need to generate with your current base64 writer. With those 3, you'll have the *bare essential* setup, and can upgrade as you please (The more reasonable setup needs 2 additional Pokemon to be generated afterwards, which will replace one of the Pokemon from the bare setup, so 4 total kind of).

The first Pokemon you'll generate is the terminal, which takes the whole 14 box names, the other two Pokemon will be smaller and take fewer box names (10 and 7/10 respectively, depending on your game).
If you're on Emerald, you have the choice between generating TRQIC and T, it's ultimately subjective, either works fine, but the Walda phrase terminal is probably preferrable.

Now there is only one last small Pokemon that needs to be generated: T×A, which is an ARM&nbsp;↔&nbsp;Thumb bootstrap. The TorchicOS terminal and all modules are written in Thumb, because it's a lot more space-efficient, but most box name codes are written in ARM. So, the species we use to execute ACE codes are also usually ones that execute the boxes as ARM, not Thumb.
Either way, this bootstrap Pokemon can be used both with ARM and Thumb entry, and it can also proceed as either Thumb or ARM, depending on its markings. If no markings are set, then the game will jump to Box 14 (Slot 3 is the first safe slot) in regular ARM mode. If any markings are set, it will execute everything after this Pokemon as Thumb (so, execute TorchicOS). **This way, you can have TorchicOS inside your boxes, but still execute regular Box name codes too by just changing the markings of this Pokemon.**
