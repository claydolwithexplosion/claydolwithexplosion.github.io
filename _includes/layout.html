Layout of TorchicOS

This is for non-Japanese Pokemon Emerald, if you are using an ARM species, such as

  • 0x410e in English Emerald
  • 0x4167 in English Emerald
  • 0x415e in Italian/German Emerald
  • 0x4130 in German Emerald
  • 0x0599 in Spanish Emerald
If you need some sort of bad egg in Box 12 to execute codes, you most likely have a Thumb species and should NOT use this layout.

With an ARM species, you can just put the three Pokemon at the start of Box 12, in this order:

  1. The Thumb/ARM Multiplexer "T×A"
  2. The Torchic terminal "T" or "TRQIC"
  3. The intermediate writer "W"

This is for non-Japanese Pokemon Emerald, if you are using a Thumb species, such as

  • 0x40e9 in English Emerald
  • 0x45bb in English Emerald
  • 0x40ed in French Emerald
